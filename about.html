About InvoiceMaker

InvoiceMaker is a free, privacy-first tool for creating professional invoices, quotes, and receipts. There's no signup, no watermark, and nothing to install — you fill in a form, watch the document build in a live preview, and download a print-ready PDF in seconds.

What InvoiceMaker does

InvoiceMaker lets you produce the paperwork small businesses and freelancers need to get paid:

  • Invoices — itemized bills with your business and client details, line items, tax, subtotal, and total due.
  • Quotes and estimates — the same clean layout for pricing a job before you start.
  • Receipts — a record of payment received, ready to hand over or email.

You also get profession-specific templates (plumber, electrician, contractor, consultant, freelancer, designer, and more) and regional formats for the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, plus free business calculators for hourly rates, sales tax, VAT, and profit margins.

Private by design

Everything you type into InvoiceMaker — your business details, client details, line items, totals, notes, and any logo you upload — is processed entirely in your web browser. Nothing you enter is uploaded to or stored on a server. When you download a PDF, the file is built on your own device using the open-source jsPDF library and saved straight to your computer. Refreshing or closing the page clears the data. This means you can invoice real clients with real figures without your data ever leaving your machine.

How it works

The site is a set of fast, self-contained static pages. Each generator renders a live preview as you type, so what you see is exactly what downloads. There are no heavy frameworks and no third-party trackers embedded in the generators themselves. We use Google Analytics for anonymous, aggregated usage statistics and Google AdSense to display ads that keep the tool free; both are described in our Privacy Policy.
